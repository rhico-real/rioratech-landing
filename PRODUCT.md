# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business decision makers and a broad company audience evaluating a technology partner for custom software and practical AI integration.

## Product Purpose

rioraTech builds custom software solutions with AI integration to make work easier for companies. The landing page should establish the company as a credible technology partner and make its services and future project work easy to understand.

## Brand Personality

Clear, traditional, and straightforward. The experience should feel credible and approachable to people with varied levels of technical knowledge.

## Anti-references

No specific anti-references were provided. The supplied screenshot is visual inspiration only; its TechMark name, copy, and identity are not rioraTech assets.

## Design Principles

- Explain technical work in plain language.
- Build confidence through a clear structure and concrete examples.
- Present AI as part of useful software solutions for companies.
- Keep the rioraTech identity distinct from reference material.
